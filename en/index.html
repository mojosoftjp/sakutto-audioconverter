Every quick audio fix, in one app.

Three everyday tasks — just switch tabs.

Even out the volume (normalize)

Bring recordings that are too quiet or too loud to a comfortable level.

  • Follows the broadcast standard EBU R128, aiming for about −16 LUFS
  • As many files as you like in one go
  • Keeps the original sample rate (up to 48 kHz for MP3, AAC and OGG)

Turn it up or down

Raise or lower the volume by exactly as many dB as you want.

  • −20 to +20 dB with a slider
  • The same change for many files at once

Convert formats

Convert to MP3, WAV, FLAC, AAC (M4A) or OGG — many files at once.

  • Bitrate (128, 192, 256, 320 kbps)
  • Sample rate (original, 44.1 or 48 kHz)
  • Also opens WMA, AIFF, Opus, AC3 and more

Trim

Cut out just the part you need, with the waveform in view.

  • Set the start and end from the current playback position
  • Type times down to 1/1000 second
  • Save as MP3, WAV, FLAC, AAC (M4A) or OGG

Install and go

FFmpeg, the engine that processes the audio, is built into the app.

  • No other software to install
  • A progress bar, and a button to stop midway
  • If a file can't be processed, the reason is shown

Originals stay as they are

Results are always saved as new files.

  • Save next to the original or in a folder you choose
  • If a name is taken, a number is added instead of overwriting

Frequently asked questions

Can it make a quiet recording easy to hear?

Yes. Choose "Normalize (auto)" in the Volume tab and press Process. The volume is evened out following the broadcast standard EBU R128, aiming for about −16 LUFS.

Can it even out the volume of many files at once?

Yes. Add the files together and they are processed one after another automatically.

Are my original files kept?

Yes. Results are always saved as new files. If a file with the same name exists, a number is added, so nothing is overwritten.

Can I turn phone recordings (M4A) into MP3?

Yes. Choose MP3 in the Convert tab to convert them in one go.

Can I cut out just part of a song or recording?

Yes. In the Trim tab, set the start and end with the waveform in view, then trim.

Can I raise or lower the volume just a little?

Yes. "Adjust (dB)" lets you set anything from −20 to +20 dB.

Does audio processing work without an internet connection?

Yes. Converting and adjusting volume happen on your PC with the FFmpeg built into the app.

Is it a one-time purchase?

Yes. Buy it once on the Microsoft Store and keep using it with no monthly fee.
